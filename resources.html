Open Tools, Data, Methods, and Training Materials

The Parker Group develops open tools, data, methods, training materials, and field-based research resources for studying health and human populations in challenging environments.

Most of these materials are maintained through our GitHub organization, where we share reproducible research repositories, software, tutorials, datasets, lab materials, and ongoing public-facing projects.
